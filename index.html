About Nova Fitness

Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Hyderabad, Nova Fitness Gym was born from a passion to democratize elite fitness. What started as a 2,000 sq.ft. training space has grown into a 15,000 sq.ft. world-class facility featuring the latest Life Fitness, Torque, and Hammer Strength equipment.

Today, we stand as one of Hyderabad's highest-rated gyms with over 2,000 active members and a team of 15+ certified fitness professionals.
